Honeyspot is a Fairfield, CT-based, five-piece band bringing a fresh, groove-driven spin to familiar songs while mixing in original tunes, blending funk, rock, and soul. Since forming in the spring of 2026, the band has performed at several local venues including Toad’s Place, Fairfield Theatre Company, Brass & Bone, Stella Blues, 314 Beer Garden, BRYAC, and Two Roads Brewing’s ‘Road Less Traveled Music Fest’.
