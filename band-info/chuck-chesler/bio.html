Chuck Chesler, the source and writer of Tyrone Shoelaces has been playing in multiple bands in the Grateful Dead world over for most of his life since beginning his career with The Lost Boys, who left their mark on the early jam band era from 1985-1995.

Chuck then began a stint with Levon Helm from 2000-2002 working alongside long time friend and musical homie, Jimmy Bennett. These sessions, helped Levon begin his journey back from cancer. They also became the prelude to the legendary Midnight Rambles.
