Mildly Brown is a Norwalk musician who made his name freestyling for hours every day on TikTok and has currently amassed 300 thousand followers doing so. His unique blend of soulfulness, and sharpness is a breath of fresh air in a world where passion seems like it’s seldomly seen. “My one goal is to demonstrate excellence on such a level that people have no choice but to believe not only in themselves, but that anything is possible”, says Brown. One moment he’s rapping like Kendrick Lamar or j cole, the next he’s singing like Justin Timberlake, and then all of a sudden he’s slapping the bass or taking a guitar solo.
