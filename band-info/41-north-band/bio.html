41° NORTH... Party-up your spot on the globe! This band brings great energy every time. It ain’t about them, it’s about you! They keep the vibe up with rock, pop and some country fun too. Dale Allen, vocal chameleon with a resonant and rich vocal tone, is “not only a singer, she’s an experience. She brings big frontwoman stage presence and crowd engagement. Breakthrough guitarist, Kenny Weiss hooks with his badass style. Connecting with every performance, his vocals cut clear and strong. Drummer, Steve Townsend switches seamlessly from shuffle, to samba to hard rock backbeat. He has recorded with The Romero Brothers, The Faker Five, and Harry Spero & The September Project, along with long-time collaborator and “in the pocket” steady-groove bass player Vince Sette. For Vinny… it’s all about the bass.
