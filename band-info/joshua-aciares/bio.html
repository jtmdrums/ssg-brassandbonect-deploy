Joshua Aciares, a native of New York, was profoundly influenced by the city’s vibrant energy and dynamic cultural landscape, which fostered his creative inclinations from an early age. His artistic journey began in his formative years, with seminal works such as John Carpenter’s The Thing igniting a deep appreciation for visual storytelling. Initially focused on illustration and design, Aciares utilized art as a medium to articulate his inner world and creative vision.

As a singer-songwriter, Aciares has developed a distinctive sound characterized by a sophisticated blend of acoustic, indie rock, experimental, and shoegaze influences. His compositions resonate with audiences on a deeply personal level, combining raw emotion with intricate melodic structures. Drawing inspiration from iconic artists such as Maynard James Keenan, Deftones, and Peter Gabriel, his music embodies a fusion of heartfelt lyricism and innovative artistry.

Throughout his professional career, Aciares has established himself as a noteworthy presence within the music industry. In 2003, he earned significant recognition as the #3 artist on Hot Topics Music, marking a pivotal milestone in his artistic journey. Further accomplishments include opening performances for prominent acts such as Wale and New Found Glory at Central Connecticut State University (CCSU) in 2010, as well as sharing the stage with renowned artist Uncle Kracker. These achievements have solidified his reputation as a skilled and versatile musician.
